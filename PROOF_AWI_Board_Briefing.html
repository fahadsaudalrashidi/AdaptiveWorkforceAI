Methodology

AWI Methodology & Assumptions

How AWI Analyses Workforce Data

AWI processes anonymised HR records through a proprietary VotingEnsemble model (CatBoost + RandomForest + GradientBoosting) trained on workforce stability indicators. The model assigns each employee a risk stability score (0–100) and classifies individuals into four risk bands. No personal identifiers are processed at any stage.

Risk Band Classification

Stable (Green): Low departure risk. Continue standard engagement.
Monitoring (Amber): Emerging signals. Periodic check-in advised.
Strategic Concern (Orange): Elevated risk. Targeted intervention recommended.
Priority Review (Red): High departure risk. Immediate leadership action required.

Financial Assumptions (SHRM Basis)

Turnover cost of SAR 120,000 per employee is sourced from SHRM Human Capital Benchmarking and represents the conservative lower bound of documented replacement cost (recruitment, onboarding, productivity loss, and knowledge transfer). This figure is applied uniformly to the at-risk cohort (Priority Review + Strategic Concern) as a conservative aggregated estimate.

Limitations

All outputs are statistical associations, not causal certainty. Risk scores reflect patterns in historical data and do not constitute a prediction of individual employee behaviour. Financial figures are estimates — not financial guarantees. Qualified HR professional review is required before any employment decision.
