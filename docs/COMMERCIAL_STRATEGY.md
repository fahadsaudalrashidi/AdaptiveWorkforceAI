## 1. PRICING PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 Value-Based Pricing Rationale
AWI is priced on **business value delivered**, not feature sets:
- Average attrition cost in Saudi private sector: SAR 18,000–45,000 per departure
- At 1,000 employees × 15% at-risk × 30% intervention success: SAR 8.1M–20.2M saved annually
- AWI Platform price: SAR 480,000/year (Professional) = 4–25× ROI on first deployment

**Price anchoring principle:** Always present cost in context of attrition exposure. AWI is not an HR software subscription — it is a risk management tool.

### 1.2 Competitive Price Floor
- Workday HCM: USD 600–800/employee/year (USD 600K–800K for 1,000 employees)
- SAP SuccessFactors: USD 500–700/employee/year
- Oracle HCM Cloud: USD 450–650/employee/year
- Visier Analytics: USD 30,000–150,000+/year (analytics add-on only)
- **AWI Government: SAR 180,000/year = USD 48,000 — 90% below Workday at full analytics depth**

## 4. LICENSING MODEL

### 4.1 License Type: Annual Subscription (SaaS-equivalent, On-Premises)
- Annual contract with 12-month minimum
- Renewal at same rate with 5% annual escalation cap
- Volume discount: 10% for 3-year pre-payment

### 4.2 License Restrictions
- Single client organisation (no sublicensing)
- On-premises deployment only (no external hosting without Enterprise add-on)
- Data isolation enforced: one deployment per tenant
- Audit trail enabled by default (cannot be disabled)
- No removal of governance disclaimers or PDPL controls

### 4.3 Academic / Research License
- SAR 18,000/year for accredited universities and research institutions
- Requires signed data governance agreement
- Excludes commercial use, client deployment, or consulting work

## 9. RISK MITIGATION

### 9.1 Commercial Risks
- **Price sensitivity in government:** Counter with Vision 2030 narrative and Saudization ROI
- **Long sales cycles:** Build pipeline 12+ months in advance; use pilot programs to accelerate
- **Build vs. buy objection:** Total cost of internal build: SAR 3M+ (4 engineers × 18 months). AWI pays back in 3 months.

### 9.2 Governance-First Positioning (Risk Reduction)
- Positioning AWI as a governance/risk tool (not just analytics) increases budget owner: CHRO + Legal + Risk Officer, not just IT
- This multi-stakeholder sale makes contracts stickier (3+ internal sponsors)
