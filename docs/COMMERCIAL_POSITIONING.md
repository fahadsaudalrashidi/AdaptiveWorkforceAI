## 2. What We Are — The Core Identity

### The Honest Definition

AWI is a **workforce stability intelligence platform** that uses theory-grounded, calibrated machine learning to help Saudi enterprises identify organizational resilience patterns at the department and unit level — enabling proactive retention strategy, not reactive HR response.

### What This Means in Practice

- HR leadership sees departmental stability trends 3–6 months before attrition concentrations appear
- Data science rigor (COR, JD-R, FFM, Mobley, Adams Equity) makes every output explainable and defensible
- PDPL-aware architecture means Saudi enterprise legal teams can evaluate the platform with confidence
- No individual employee data in any output means the platform is deployable without employee consent concerns for aggregate analytics

## 4. Target Customer Profile

### Primary Buyer: CHRO / Chief People Officer

**Profile:**
- Saudi or GCC organization, 1,000–50,000 employees
- Facing elevated attrition in knowledge-intensive or operational roles
- Under pressure from leadership to improve workforce retention
- Aware of PDPL requirements and concerned about data handling
- Seeking analytics that are defensible to HR ethics committees

**Key concern:** "We need workforce analytics that won't create legal risk, can be explained to employees, and won't make decisions without HR review."

**AWI answer:** Every output includes mandatory governance disclaimers. No individual employee data. Full theory explainability. PDPL technical controls. HR professional review required for every recommendation.

### Secondary Buyer: CIO / CTO

**Profile:**
- Responsible for enterprise data architecture
- Needs a platform that fits Saudi data residency requirements
- Wants multi-tenant isolation for a portfolio of subsidiaries
- Concerned about vendor lock-in and architecture auditability

**Key concern:** "Can this be deployed in our Saudi data center? Is it auditable? Does it isolate subsidiary data?"

**AWI answer:** Docker-deployable. Saudi data center runbook included. Structural tenant isolation (not configuration-based). Full audit trail. 11 production governance locks in code.

### Tertiary Buyer: CFO / Board Member

**Profile:**
- Evaluating investment in workforce analytics
- Wants defensible ROI projections
- Concerned about regulatory exposure
- Wants to understand competitive differentiation

**Key concern:** "What is the financial justification? What is our regulatory exposure?"

**AWI answer:** ROI estimates cite CIPD Middle East HR Benchmark 2023 and SHRM 2022 replacement cost multipliers. Regulatory exposure framing: platform reduces PDPL data handling risk compared to ad-hoc HR analytics.

## 9. Investor Narrative

### The Opportunity
Saudi enterprises collectively employ millions in structured organizations. Workforce instability costs Saudi enterprises an estimated 1.5–2.0x annual salary per departed employee in replacement costs (CIPD Middle East HR Benchmark 2023). Proactive analytics that reduce attrition by even 5–10 percentage points generates significant financial value at enterprise scale.

### The Defensibility
- Scientific governance pipeline: competitors cannot replicate the theory-grounding and calibration rigor quickly
- PDPL-native architecture: first-mover advantage in Saudi regulatory compliance
- Multi-tenant SaaS: scalable architecture that supports portfolio companies and government ministries
- No individual employee outputs: structurally reduces the regulatory and reputational risks that have plagued other HR AI vendors globally

### The Risk Disclosure
- Not yet deployed to live customers
- JWT authentication pending implementation
- Persistent storage pending infrastructure integration
- Legal review of PDPL compliance pending
- Independent security audit pending
- ROI projections based on published benchmarks, not historical platform performance

## 10. Brand Guidelines

### Visual Identity Principles
- **Minimal**: No clutter, no decorative elements without information value
- **Premium**: Enterprise-grade spacing, typography, and data density
- **Trustworthy**: Blue primary palette; clear hierarchy; no aggressive red
- **Arabic-native**: RTL support, Cairo font, right-aligned layouts in Arabic mode
- **Governance-visible**: Compliance indicators always present, never hidden

### Color Semantics (maintain across all materials)
| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Blue (brand primary) | Platform identity, navigation, trust |
| Emerald green | Stable, active, healthy |
| Amber | Monitoring, caution, attention |
| Orange | Strategic concern, elevated |
| Red | Priority review, critical |
| Gold accent | Premium KPIs, executive-tier data |

### Typography
- English: Inter (system-safe fallback: system-ui)
- Arabic: Cairo (system-safe fallback: system-ui)
- Numeric data: tabular-nums for alignment

### Tone of Voice
- **Confident, not certain**: "Elevated indicators" not "will resign"
- **Scientific, not technical**: Theory references accessible, not jargon-heavy
- **Cautious, not alarming**: Risk bands named for action, not alarm
- **Honest, not over-promising**: All limitations disclosed; no legal compliance claims
