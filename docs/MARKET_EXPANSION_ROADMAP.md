## STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Build AWI from an academic-grade analytics prototype into a commercially viable national workforce intelligence platform valued at SAR 50M+ within 36 months.

This roadmap defines the four-stage maturity arc, revenue projections, pricing architecture, and strategic milestones required to achieve this outcome.

## PRICING ARCHITECTURE

### Pricing Principles
1. Value-based pricing (priced against attrition cost avoided, not features)
2. Entry-point accessible (pilot tier to remove first-purchase barrier)
3. Expansion revenue model (start small, expand to full platform)
4. Partner margin built in (40% margin for resellers/implementation partners)

### Tier 1: PILOT
**SAR 45,000 / 30-day engagement**  
Target: First-time buyers, proof-of-concept, budget exploration

Includes:
- One full workforce analysis (unlimited employees)
- Executive Index Dashboard (WSI, RHI, TFRI, LSI, ORI, HCRI)
- Board-ready PDF report (EN + AR)
- ROI estimate for top 2 interventions
- Digital twin: top 3 scenarios
- 30-day access to platform
- 1 onboarding call (2 hours)
- Email support

*Converts to Annual subscription at discounted first-year rate.*

---

### Tier 2: PROFESSIONAL
**SAR 180,000 / year**  
Target: Mid-size enterprises, 500–5,000 employees, CHRO buyers

Includes everything in Pilot, plus:

- Historical trend analysis (analysis-to-analysis comparison)
- Industry benchmark comparison (Saudi sector)
- Integration: SFTP auto-ingestion

- Priority email support (48-hour response)
- Quarterly business review (virtual)

**SAR 480,000 / year**  
Target: Large enterprises, 5,000+ employees, Fortune 500 Saudi

- SLA: 99.9% uptime, 4-hour critical support response
- On-site implementation support (2 days)

- Annual renewal with 10% price lock

## LICENSING MODELS

### Model 1: SaaS Subscription (Primary)
Annual subscription, per-tier pricing above. 80% of revenue target.

### Model 2: Per-Analysis Licensing
For occasional users (recruitment firms, consulting firms):
- **Single Analysis: SAR 8,000** — one upload, one full report, 30-day access
- **Analysis Pack (5): SAR 30,000** — 5 analyses, 90-day access
- For: Due diligence, M&A workforce assessment, project-based consulting

### Model 3: Embedded API Licensing
For partners embedding AWI in their own platforms:
- **API Call Pricing: SAR 8 per employee scored**
- **Minimum: SAR 15,000/month**
- Revenue share with integration partner: 30% to partner
- Target: SAP, Oracle, regional HRIS vendors

### Model 4: White-Label Partner Licensing
For Big 4 or consulting firms deploying AWI under their brand:
- **License Fee: SAR 120,000/year base** + 25% of client fees
- Includes: white-labeling, partner training, co-branded reports
- Target: Deloitte KSA, KPMG KSA, McKinsey KSA, PwC KSA

### Model 5: Government Subscription
National-level platform for ministry/authority clients:
- **National License: SAR 2.5M–5M/year** (flat fee, unlimited employees)
- Includes: source code escrow, government cloud deployment, SLA 99.99%
- Target: MOHRSD, HRDF, General Authority for Statistics, PIF
