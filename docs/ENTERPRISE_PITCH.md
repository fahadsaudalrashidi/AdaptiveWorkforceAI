## The Problem

**Employee attrition is expensive, predictable, and largely preventable — yet most organisations manage it reactively.**

- Replacing one employee costs 50–200% of annual salary (SHRM, Gallup benchmarks)
- HR leaders have access to data but rarely have time or tools to derive actionable insight from it
- Commercial AI HR tools require cloud data uploads, creating data residency, privacy, and regulatory risk
- Most analytics platforms show dashboards — they tell you what happened, not who is at risk and what to do
- Black-box models produce recommendations with no explainability, making them unusable for governance-conscious organisations
- Arabic-language HR markets are systemically underserved by English-only tools

---

## The Solution

**A fully local, offline workforce intelligence platform that:**

1. Accepts any standard HR CSV/Excel file — no required format, no schema lock-in
2. Scores every employee on a 0–100 attrition risk scale using six established organisational behaviour theories
3. Validates predictive quality using conservative machine learning — honest metrics, not inflated benchmarks
4. Generates board-level decision intelligence: Executive Priority Score, strategic priorities, decision Q&A, department briefs
5. Simulates the estimated impact of HR interventions before investment
6. Delivers all outputs in English and Arabic with embedded governance disclaimers
7. Requires no cloud, no subscription, no data transfer — everything runs on the client's machine

## Why Now

**Three converging forces create an urgent market window:**

1. **AI governance regulation is accelerating.** The EU AI Act classifies HR AI systems as high-risk. Saudi PDPL (Personal Data Protection Law) imposes strict data residency and processing constraints. Organisations need AI tools they can justify to regulators — not black boxes.

2. **Saudi Vision 2030 is driving Saudisation at scale.** The nationalisation programme is producing urgent workforce planning demand across government, healthcare, education, and private sector — all with Arabic-language requirements commercial tools do not meet.

3. **Post-pandemic attrition normalisation is reversing.** After "quiet quitting" and the Great Resignation, organisations are rebuilding retention programmes — but now with heightened board scrutiny on HR investment ROI. They need evidence, not dashboards.

## AI Governance Advantage

**Governance is the moat.**

Regulators, boards, and legal teams are increasingly asking: "How does your HR AI make decisions, and can you justify them?"

Adaptive Workforce AI is built to answer that question at every layer:

- **No causal certainty:** The system never says "X causes attrition." It says "X is statistically associated with higher attrition risk." This distinction is enforced in code, not policy.
- **No employment decision automation:** The system explicitly prohibits use as the sole basis for hiring, firing, promotion, or demotion decisions. This is displayed on every output page.
- **Transparent feature exclusion:** Every ML run documents which columns were excluded and why (PII, identifiers, proxy leakage). Full audit trail.
- **Conservative metrics:** We report the honest result (AUC 0.82) rather than the inflated result (AUC 1.0). This builds trust with technical reviewers.
- **Bilingual disclaimers:** Governance notes appear in Arabic and English — addressing the workforce population, not just the analytics team.

This governance posture is a procurement advantage with government, healthcare, and regulated industry buyers.
